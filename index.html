Calculation policy

Every realized losing sale consumes capacity for its closing session and the following trading sessions until it rolls out. Profitable sales show in history, but do not erase losses. Every recorded net CAD profit or loss also adjusts the portfolio's tracked equity and therefore changes its percentage risk ceiling. Closing a position releases its unused open reserve immediately. Entry debit is never counted as a realized loss.

WHY TWO DIFFERENT NUMBERS?

Stress reserve vs. maximum debit

A $420 CAD option debit with a 70% stress assumption reserves roughly $300. Its purchased premium remains the actual maximum loss if it goes to zero. This app does not pretend an estimated exit is guaranteed.

Your own invalidation and time stop need to be decided separately, before entry.
