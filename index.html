Trade history & cash outcomes

The ledger stays the same width as the rest of your workspace. On smaller tablets, swipe inside the table. Thesis and catalyst wrap naturally at every screen width, while long notes in other columns wrap on smaller screens. Phones show one card per fill. Return % is net realized P/L divided by the entry cost of contracts closed, including fees. It is blank until an exit and cannot be calculated for manual P/L adjustments without cost basis.

TP, invalidation, and thesis reflect the latest recorded plan (not a backdated entry plan). Use Edit plan on any history row to update thesis, catalyst, trigger, targets and invalidation; dated changes remain in the timeline. IBKR does not supply your thesis or underlying levels.

Calculation policy

Within the five-session window, partial exits from the same continuous position attempt are netted before charging a loss. Profits from separate trades do not offset losses, and a new entry after fully closing is a new attempt. Open contracts continue reserving risk. Every net realized CAD result changes tracked equity and the percentage risk ceiling. Return % uses the allocated purchase cost of only the contracts closed, with fees included; entries and manual P/L adjustments without a purchase cost show ----.

YOUR OPERATING RULES

Portfolio settings

Edit anytime, recalculated live

Idea risk policy: Fixed mode flags planned or open ideas above the CAD per-idea guide; it is advisory, not a trade-entry block. Flexible mode removes that optional guide and lets one or several ideas use the available rolling risk pool. Neither setting changes the portfolio's five-session risk ceiling, the actual risk reserves, or existing trade records.

Commission estimates, not broker facts: New entries, exits, and size-planner drafts use these per-contract USD defaults to prefill each order's total fees. Change the amount on any trade. IBKR CSV imports supply actual execution commissions and replace matched estimated fees; they never use these defaults as actual broker records. Buy default is illustrative; the sell estimate is provisional and actual broker fees vary by execution.

The 3% limit uses tracked realized equity. Change this amount only to reconcile realized equity (excluding unrealized option P/L) or account transfers; past results stay logged.

WHY TWO DIFFERENT NUMBERS?

Stress reserve vs. maximum debit

A $420 CAD option debit with a 70% stress assumption reserves roughly $300. Its purchased premium remains the actual maximum loss if it goes to zero. This app does not pretend an estimated exit is guaranteed.

Your own invalidation and time stop need to be decided separately, before entry.
